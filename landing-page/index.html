FAQs

Questions, answered.

How do you scope and price projects?

We start with a free 30-minute call to understand the work. From there, we send a written proposal with fixed scope, milestones, and pricing — usually within 2 business days.

Do you offer ongoing support after delivery?

Yes. Most clients move into a managed service plan after launch. We don't disappear when the build is done — that's when the real work starts.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Most projects run 4–12 weeks of focused build time, followed by a monthly retainer for support and improvements. Custom engagements are scoped per situation.

Can you work alongside our existing team?

Often, yes. We frequently embed with internal teams as senior contractors, advisors, or fractional leads. Your engineers don't need to be replaced — they need leverage.

Do you sign NDAs?

Always. Send yours over and we'll sign the same day, or use ours.

Where are you based?

Our team is based in Canada and works with clients across North America. Remote-first, with on-site engagements when it matters.
